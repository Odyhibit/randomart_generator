Randomart Etch-a-Sketch

← Back to the Randomart Visualizer
Every move the bishop makes comes from 2 bits: 4 possible diagonal directions. Click them yourself, one at a time — like an Etch-A-Sketch that can never lift its pen — and watch both the picture and the exact bytes that would produce it build up together.

How this works

Every 4 clicks complete one byte — your first click becomes that byte's lowest 2 bits, your fourth click becomes its highest 2 bits (the walk always consumes bits low-end-first; see docs/algorithm.md). Once you've completed a byte, you can click Finish (place E) to mark the current position as the end of the walk, exactly like a real randomart box — or keep drawing right past it.
